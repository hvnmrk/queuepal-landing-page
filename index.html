Yes. QueuePal is currently offered as a free download for Android.

Use the download button on this website. It links to an official APK published in the QueuePal landing page's GitHub Releases.

Not yet. QueuePal is currently distributed through a direct Android APK download.

Download the APK on an Android device, open the downloaded file, and follow the installation instructions. Your device may ask you to allow installations from that source.

Can I use QueuePal for multiple courts?

Yes. QueuePal includes court management functionality for organizing sessions with multiple courts.
